The Plasma Gasifier

For non-organic waste, our **Innovation Lab** (Page 62) has implemented small-scale plasma gasification. This process uses extreme heat to break down molecules into "syngas" for electricity and an inert glass-like "slag" used for **Pillar House** (Page 33) construction materials.

Nutrient Recovery

Our **Wellness Division** (Page 46) ensures that all organic matter from our kitchens and gardens is processed into nutrient-rich fertilizers. This "Black Gold" is returned directly to the **Sovereign Farms**, creating a perfect cycle of health that starts and ends within our borders.
