OFFICE OF MEDIATION

Conflict Resolution & Restorative Justice

RESTORING THE VILLAGE

The Bureau Judiciary provides a sovereign alternative for dispute resolution, emphasizing community restoration over punitive litigation.

01

Notice of Dispute

Citizens or Vendors file a formal notice through the Bureau of Records. This triggers a stay on any community-based actions until mediation is completed.

02

The Council of Pillars

A panel of three impartial, high-ranking Pillars from the local Philadelphia or Minneapolis hub is assigned to review the evidence and hear all perspectives.

03

The Restorative Agreement

Instead of fines or jail, the Council focuses on "Making it Right." This may involve labor, reimbursement, or public service to the Village Infrastructure.

Request Bureau Mediation

For internal disputes, vendor disagreements, or neighborhood grievances.
