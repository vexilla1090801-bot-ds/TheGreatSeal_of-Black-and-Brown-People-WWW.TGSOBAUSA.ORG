NOTICE OF PROPRIETARY INTEREST & NATIONAL ASSET STATUS

I. Protection of the Great Seal

The **Great Seal of Black and Brown People of the United States** (as represented by logos Tgsobabpousa1.png and Tgsobabpousa2.png) is the intellectual property of Thin Black & Brown Lines USA Nonprofit Corporation. It is a monumental documentation of a resilient nation's identity.

"Unauthorized reproduction, digital manipulation, or commercial exploitation of these seals constitutes an infringement of federal copyright law. All identity certifications issued by the Bureau are licensed specifically to the individual for non-transferable use."

II. Registry of Existence & AI-Identity

TBBLUSA.ORG maintains a proprietary Registry of Existence. This registry functions as a digital patent for the human identity of our community members, protecting their likeness and heritage from unauthorized AI data-scraping and systemic dehumanization.

III. The Association of Success

We operate within a "Family of Corporations"—a collaborative association that provides the legal and financial back-end for our solution-based initiatives. This association ensures that every grant, contract, and donation is protected by a multi-layered corporate charter designed for community preservation.

IV. Licensing & Partner Certification

Other nonprofits or government agencies wishing to utilize the TBBL framework or display the Great Seal must apply for a Credentialed Partner License. This ensures that the high standard of "Goodness" and "Positivity" is maintained across all chapters, from Philadelphia to Minnesota.
