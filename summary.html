02. Infrastructure

Physical 'Pillar Houses' in Philadelphia and Minneapolis acting as hubs for food, medical aid, and emergency response.

03. Economy

A centralized Sovereign Fund and Procurement Portal circulating capital within the local vendor ecosystem.
