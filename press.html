Thin Black & Brown Lines USA Announces National Bureau Opening

PHILADELPHIA, PA — Today marks the official activation of the Bureau of National Identity and Sovereign Records. Following a successful pilot in Philadelphia, the organization is expanding its "Pillar House" infrastructure to Minneapolis, MN, providing the first proprietary identity certifications for Black and Brown Americans.

"We are moving beyond conversation into solution-based documentation," says the Bureau Executive. "The Great Seal represents our commitment to systemic excellence and community sovereignty."

"The expansion from Philadelphia to Minnesota is not just a geographic move; it is a national proof-of-concept for community-led governance and identity protection."
