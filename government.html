Capability Statement & Federal Relations

TBBLUSA.ORG serves as a Solution-Based Prime Contractor and Partner for Federal and State Agencies. Our mission is to infuse community ecosystems with data-driven identity protection and social justice infrastructure. We represent a national network of "Pillars"—Black and Brown citizens engaged in systemic improvement.

Core Competencies

  • National Identity Documentation: High-security verification of citizens.
  • Civic Infrastructure: Building safe-zones and "Pillar House" centers.
  • Economic Restoration: Training in government contracting and bid management.
  • Justice Reform: Verified "Goodness Code" re-entry programs.
