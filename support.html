Common Inquiries

How long does the verification process take?

Once your documentation is uploaded to the Bureau of Records, our regional directors typically complete verification within 5-7 business days.

Can I use my Great Seal Identity for federal applications?

While our certifications are proprietary to the Bureau, they are designed to align with federal "Capability Statements" to assist you in government contracting and grant procurement.

Is my data safe from AI scraping?

Yes. Every record in the Citizen Vault is protected by our AI-Identity Shield, which utilizes Sovereign-Village-OS protocols to prevent unauthorized data mining.
