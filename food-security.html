The Nutrient-Density Standard

The Bureau doesn't just store calories; we store **vitality**. Our strategic reserves are monitored by the **Wellness Division** (Page 46) to ensure the stored crops retain 95% of their nutrient density for up to five years. This includes our proprietary "Sovereign Seed Bank"—a collection of non-GMO, heritage seeds ready for immediate planting if local crops are compromised.
